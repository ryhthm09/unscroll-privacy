Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Unscroll helps you avoid YouTube Shorts by detecting supported Shorts screens and attempting to return you to YouTube Home. This policy describes the current Android app in clear, practical terms.

Accessibility access

Unscroll asks you to enable an Android AccessibilityService so it can recognize a YouTube Shorts player and activate a safe Home control. Android delivers YouTube window events to the service. The service examines the on-screen accessibility structure, including interface identifiers, labels or content descriptions, control state, visibility, and positions. It uses this information on your device to decide whether a redirect is safe and to verify that YouTube Home appeared. If it cannot verify the screen or target, it may leave the screen unchanged.

You can use the protection switch in Unscroll without changing Android's permission, or revoke the service access in Android Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps → Unscroll protection. Settings names may vary by device.

Accessibility information is used while the service handles an event. Unscroll does not save YouTube video titles, search terms, watch history, screenshots, or the accessibility tree to its app storage. The current app does not record your screen or capture audio. A temporary reminder banner may appear after a verified redirect if Mindful reminders are enabled.

Information stored on your device

Unscroll stores these items in its private app preferences:

  • Whether Shorts protection and Mindful reminders are enabled, and whether you accepted the accessibility explanation.
  • A small reminder rotation state, so messages can vary.
  • Verified redirect counts: an all-time total and counts by local calendar day for the recent reporting window (about 90 days).
  • Your selected estimate of 15, 30, or 60 seconds per redirect.
  • A bounded set of recent numeric encounter IDs to prevent duplicate counts. These IDs do not contain YouTube content.

A redirect is counted only after Unscroll verifies the return to YouTube Home. An accessibility event, detection attempt, retry, or unverified navigation is not counted as a successful skip. Daily counts use the device's local date at the time of the verified redirect; changing time zones can change which local day receives a future count. Older daily entries may leave the reporting window, while the all-time total remains until app data is cleared.

Potential time reclaimed is an estimate, not measured viewing time. It is the number of verified redirects multiplied by your selected 15-, 30-, or 60-second assumption. Unscroll does not measure how long you would have watched a Short.

Diagnostics, sharing, and other services

The app has no account, backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or Android INTERNET permission. It does not automatically send accessibility information, settings, or statistics to us. Debug builds can write timing, structural identifiers, and the accessibility description of a clicked control to Android's local diagnostic log. Release builds do not emit these app debug logs. Unscroll does not automatically attach logs to support emails.

If you choose Contact support, Unscroll opens an email app with our address and a suggested subject. You decide what to write and send; your email provider handles that message under its own privacy terms. If you choose a published privacy-policy link, your browser handles that page under its own terms. YouTube and Android operate independently of Unscroll.

Backup, retention, and deletion

Android backup is disabled for Unscroll, and its shared preferences are excluded from the app's configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Unscroll does not keep a server-side copy. Settings and counts remain in app storage until you change them, clear Unscroll's app data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the locally stored Unscroll information. Turning off protection or revoking accessibility access stops the feature but does not erase existing counts and preferences.

Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy if Unscroll's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions or support, email unscroll.team@gmail.com.
